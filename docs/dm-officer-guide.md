# DM Officer guide

As a DM officer you work the tickets assigned to you and record what you did. The app is designed to work well on a phone.

## Signing in
Sign in with the email your administrator set up. You land on **My Tickets**. If you see *"Your account isn't linked to an officer yet"*, ask an administrator to link your login to your officer name.

## Your queue
The cards at the top show your totals: **Open**, **In progress**, **Overdue**, **Due today** and **Resolved this month**. Tap a card to jump to that list.

Tabs:
- **Active** — everything still open or in progress, soonest due first.
- **Open / In progress / Overdue / Resolved** — as named.

Each ticket card has a coloured edge and a badge:
- 🔴 **Red** — overdue.
- 🟠 **Amber** — due today or within 2 days.
- 🟢 **Green** — on track.

New assignments appear immediately and the 🔔 bell shows a red counter.

## Your debtor list

**My Debtor List** in the sidebar is the monthly call list of customers who owe money. It works the same way as your tickets: cards at the top, accounts below, call and WhatsApp on every card. Open an account to record the amount collected, the feedback reason and a comment. The full walkthrough is in the [debt recovery guide](debt-recovery-guide.md).

## Contacting the client
Every card has **Call** (opens your phone dialler), **WhatsApp** (opens a chat with a greeting that includes the ticket number) and **SMS** (sends a text through the company's SMS account — pick a template, check the preview, send).

## Working a ticket
1. Tap **Start work** on the card (or open the ticket and press **Start work**). Status changes to **In-Progress**.
2. Open the ticket (tap the card). In **Progress**:
   - **REMARKS** — what you did and the outcome. Required before resolving.
   - **COMPLETION DATE** — leave empty to use the moment you resolve, or set the actual date/time if you finished earlier.
   - **Payment fields** — for payment issues, confirm or correct PAYMENT, PAYMENT SOURCE, PAYMENT TYPE and PAYMENT STATUS.
3. Press **Save changes** to save notes without changing the status, or **Mark resolved** when done.

A supervisor then reviews and **closes** the ticket. If more work is needed they reopen it and you are notified.

## Overdue tickets
If a ticket is past its DUE DATE and not yet resolved, you must fill **REASON FOR OPEN STATUS** (for example *"Awaiting confirmation from Finance"*) before saving. Update it whenever the situation changes — supervisors read it in reports.

## The assistant

The **Assistant** tab on a ticket shows how issues like this one were resolved before: the typical time taken, who usually handles them, and the actual notes officers wrote. Use it when an issue is unfamiliar. If your administrator has turned on AI suggestions, **Draft with AI** also writes a short script you can read to the customer — always check it before using it.

## Comments and attachments
Use **Comments** for internal notes (visible to anyone who can see the ticket) and **Attachments** for receipts, screenshots or letters (images, PDF, Word, Excel up to 10 MB). You can delete files you uploaded.

## What you can't change
Client details, issue type, due date and assignment are managed by agents and supervisors. If something is wrong, add a comment explaining the correction needed.

## Tips
- Add the app to your phone's home screen from the browser menu for one-tap access.
- Work overdue tickets first, then those due today.
- **History** on a ticket shows every change and who made it.
- Your resolution time is measured from when the ticket was raised to when you mark it resolved, so start work on tickets as soon as you can.
